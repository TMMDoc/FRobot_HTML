How the gripper is chosen

How the gripper is chosen

When FRobot creates a solution, it chooses a gripper for the part from the gripper catalog. You do not have to pick one, but you can: see Choosing a gripper yourself.

The rule

A gripper can be used for a part when all of these hold:

  1. It has suction cups.

  2. Its cups suit the material. Magnetic cups are used only on a magnetic material.

  3. It fits the part. Its cup layout, cups included, fits within the part’s bounding rectangle, one way round or the other.

  4. Every cup sits on material. At the grip point each cup lies wholly on the sheet, not over a hole or past the edge.

    • FRobot tries the gripper turned by 0°, 180°, 90° and 270°, in that order, on top of the grip angle.

    • Once you have used Next heading, it keeps your heading and tries no turns.

  5. Its cups hold the part’s weight, with a margin. FRobot adds up the cups' rated payloads (in newtons), divides by 8 for safety and by 10 to get kilograms. A gripper can set its own safety factor instead of 8.

  6. The robot can carry it. The part plus the gripper must stay within the robot’s payload. This is skipped for a robot without a payload (FanucX).

Of the grippers that can be used, FRobot takes:

  1. the cell’s own gripper, if it is one of them;

  2. otherwise the one with the lowest priority number in the catalog;

  3. then the one with the largest cup layout;

  4. then by name.

When it chooses

FRobot chooses a gripper:

  • when you click Pick & Place Tech;

  • when you open a cell config while a part is open;

  • when you switch the robot while a solution is open;

  • when you click Choose for the part in the Gripper panel.

It does not choose again:

  • when you open a solution (.fr), which keeps its gripper;

  • when you click recompute;

  • when you edit waypoints or the pickup;

  • once you have picked a gripper by hand. New solutions on the same screen keep that gripper until you:

    • click Choose for the part;

    • open a cell config;

    • switch the robot;

    • or open another part or solution.

What it tells you

A toast names the choice:

Toast Meaning

Gripper Vac_Grip chosen for 2Fold (3.53 kg): holds 3.8 kg

Chosen for this part. A turned grip is added, for example , the grip turned 90°.

Gripper …, the cell’s own, kept for …

The cell’s gripper can be used, so it stays.

Gripper … kept for … : assigned by hand (or : as saved in the solution)

You picked this gripper yourself, or the solution brought it, so it stays.

No grippers found for … (… kg): reason. … kept

No gripper in the catalog can be used, for the reason given. The mounted one stays; check the part and the catalog.

The Gripper panel also says why the mounted gripper is there: see Choosing a gripper yourself.
