Setting up the cell

Setting up the cell

A cell is the robot and everything around it: the pallet the parts come from, the stations they visit, and any other fixtures. FRobot plans the robot’s moves between the stations and keeps clear of everything else.

The robot

FRobot works with FANUC 6-axis robots. These ship with it:

Robot Payload

LR10iA_10

10 kg

M20iD_25

25 kg

M710iD_50M

50 kg

FanucX

a demonstration robot without a payload limit

The payload counts when FRobot chooses a gripper: the part plus the gripper must not be heavier than the robot can carry.

Each robot has its own cell configs. The one named Default is loaded when FRobot starts on that robot. FanucX and M710iD_50M ship without a Default config.

Stations and roles

Every cell element has a role. The role tells FRobot what the robot does there:

Role What the robot does there Phase in the cycle

Pickup

Picks the part from the top of the stack. It must be a pallet: see below.

P

Centering Table

Puts the part down, so it slides into the table’s corner, and picks it up again: the part’s position is then known exactly.

C

Deposit

Puts the part down, one place after another in a grid.

D

Overturner

Puts the part down and picks it up from the other side, when it has to be turned over: in a cell with a bending machine, when Flip part is ticked in the Deposit panel.

R (regrip)

Other

Nothing: an obstacle the robot keeps clear of.

Double-Sheet Detector

Nothing yet: an obstacle.

Tool Changer

Nothing yet: an obstacle.

A machine from the machine library is a station of its own. If it has a bed, the robot loads the part onto it (L), waits while it is bent, and unloads it (U).

Note
 Only a pallet can be the pickup: a FlexCell Pallet or Pallet V2 from the library, or your own component with a Type=Pallet .ini file. FRobot uses the first pallet with the role Pickup, otherwise the first pallet of any role. Giving another element the role Pickup does not make it a pickup.

Station user frames

Each element sits at its station user frame: a position X, Y, Z and a rotation W, P, R, as a FANUC controller would hold them, measured in the robot’s world frame. The frame is the element’s placement: change the frame and the element moves.

The world frame’s origin lies on the robot’s first axis, at the height of its second axis, as on the FANUC controller. Frames are drawn as red, green and blue axes (X, Y, Z):

  • the Display panel’s switches show or hide the world frame and the station frames;

  • View ▸ World Frame shows or hides the world frame only.
