The 3D model

The 3D model

Click 3D Model in the Workflow window. There is no dialog:

  • Thickness: 1 mm, or the thickness given in a GEO file.

  • Stack: the pallet holds a stack of 5 parts.

  • Folding: a drawing with bend lines is shown folded, as the machine will bend it. Without bend lines it is a flat sheet.

FRobot shows the 3D part on its own, centred. The Workflow window shows its name, for example 2Fold — folded, 2 bends, and offers the next step, Pick & Place Tech.

Note
 The robot picks the part flat from the stack. In a cell with a bending machine, the part is bent there and put down folded. Without a machine, it stays flat all through the cycle.

Messages from the drawing

A drawing with stray lines or unclosed shapes still makes a part where it can. FRobot notes what it left out in its status line, which you read at the foot of the Part panel (settings ▸ Part):

Message Meaning

(n tiny loops ignored)

Very small closed shapes were skipped.

(complex DXF — extruded outer profile + N holes)

The outline and its holes were used.

(complex DXF — extruded outer silhouette only)

Only the outer outline was used; holes were left out.

When no part can be made, the flat drawing disappears and the view stays empty. Click 2D Part to see the drawing again. The status line says why:

Message What to do

'file' has no closed profile to extrude

The drawing needs a closed outer outline. Close it in your CAD program.

… could not be extruded (no valid profile)

Check the outline for overlaps and gaps.

A GEO file that can’t be read already fails when you open it, with 2D open failed: … is not a GEO file or … has no cut contour. The file is damaged, not a GEO file, or has no outline to cut.

Going back to the drawing

2D Part in the Workflow window goes back to the flat drawing.

Note
 This drops the 3D part and the solution, without asking. Save the solution first if you want to keep it.
