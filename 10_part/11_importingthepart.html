Importing the part

The part the robot handles comes from its flat drawing: a DXF (.dxf) or GEO (.geo) file.

Opening the drawing

There are three ways:

  • File ▸ Open…​, or open on the rail, choosing the .dxf or .geo file;

  • add ▸ 2D Sheet (DXF / GEO);

  • 2D Part in the Workflow window, when no part is open yet.

The drawing is shown flat, seen from above, and the cell is hidden while you look at it.

  • The bottom bar shows the part’s name.

  • The rail gains measure and workflow. The Workflow window does not open by itself; click workflow to go on.

If the screen already holds a part, a solution or an unsaved new cell, the drawing opens on a new screen with a block of its own in the bottom bar. See Several solutions at once.

Note
 A drawing is not added to the recent files under File. Only solutions and cell configs are.

Bend lines

Bend lines are drawn green and dashed, each with its bend angle:

  • dashed for a bend up;

  • dash-dot-dot for a bend down.

FRobot finds them as follows:

In a A bend line is

GEO file

a bend line of the file, with its angle

DXF file

a line with a BEND_ANGLE: text

DXF file

a line on a layer named BEND, BIEG or FOLD. The angle comes from the layer name or the nearest number in the drawing; without either it is 90° up.

The 3D model is folded along these lines: see The 3D model.

Material and weight

FRobot reads the material from the drawing:

  • the GEO file’s header;

  • a MATERIAL: text in a DXF.

The density comes from the GEO file, or from the material’s name. Without a material, FRobot takes steel at 7.85 kg/dm³.

The material matters for two things:

  • the part’s weight, which the gripper must be able to hold;

  • magnetic grippers, which are only used on a magnetic material.

See How the gripper is chosen.

Note
 The material can’t be changed in FRobot. Set it in the drawing.
