When FRobot creates a solution, it places the stack on the pickup pallet:

  • Where: the part’s corner lies on the pallet’s corner, along the pallet’s axes.

  • Grip point: the robot picks the part at the centre of its bounding rectangle, on top of the stack.

To change how the part lies and how it is gripped, click the part on the pallet. The robot moves to the pickup, and the Pickup panel opens.

Control What it does

Position X, Y

Moves the part on the pallet, in mm (the arrows step 10 mm)

Angle

Turns the part about its pickup point, in degrees (the arrows step 5°)

Align corner

Moves the part, at its present angle, back against the pallet’s corner

Next heading

Turns the gripper on the part to the next heading that works, in steps of 90°

Layers

The number of parts in the stack

Simulate

Scrubs through the cycle: the number is the step, the slider moves through it

Waypoints

Opens the Waypoints panel on the phase the robot is in: Waypoints

RG-Stations

Opens the Frames panel: Frames and roles

Pallet

Zooms the view out to the whole cell

Enter applies a typed value; the arrows apply at once.

Note
 Every change here plans the cycle again, so waypoints you edited by hand are lost. Make these changes before you fine-tune waypoints.

The grip angle

To save the robot’s wrist from turning, FRobot may turn the grip on the part by 90° or 180°. It does not do this when you chose the heading with Next heading.

Next heading reports Next heading: gripper turned to N° on the part, or that no other heading works. The message shows in the status line of the Part panel (settings ▸ Part), not in the Pickup panel.

Note
 Flip part, Table and Double station have no effect at the pickup in this version.

The same panel at the other stations

Click the part at another station to adjust it there. The same panel opens under that station’s name:

Panel Opens when you click What differs

Centering

the part on the centering table

the part’s place and angle on the table; its gripper panel offers Use pickup grip (pick it up again with the same grip)

Deposit

the folded part on the deposit, in a cell with a bending machine

Flip part works here and turns the part over

Regrip

the folded part on the Overturner

its place on the Overturner; Centre on the cups centres it on the Overturner’s cups

In a cell without a bending machine, the part is put down flat, and clicking it opens the Pickup panel.

Note
 Flip part needs an Overturner in the cell, where the robot can take the part from its other side. Without one, the phase strip reports that the part would have to be turned over in the gripper.
