FRobot User Manual

Plan, check and program a robot that picks sheet-metal parts from a pallet and puts them down where they belong.

FRobot plans the pick-and-place cycle of a FANUC 6-axis robot in a sheet-metal cell. You give it the part (a flat drawing) and the cell (the robot and its stations). FRobot then does five things:

  • it chooses a gripper that can hold the part;

  • it plans every move of the robot;

  • it checks the moves for collisions and reach;

  • it lets you watch the cycle in 3D;

  • it writes the robot program.

Conventions

  • Menu paths are written with ▸: File ▸ New ▸ Create New Cell.

  • Buttons on the tool rail at the left are named as they are labelled there, in lower case: open, workflow, nc code.

  • Names on screen (buttons, fields, window titles) are in bold, exactly as FRobot shows them.

  • Units: lengths are in millimetres and angles in degrees.

  • Robot coordinates follow the FANUC convention: X, Y, Z for the position and W, P, R for the rotation.

Note
 The pictures in this manual were made with the shipped samples, on the M20iD_25 robot and its default cell. Your cells and parts will look different.
