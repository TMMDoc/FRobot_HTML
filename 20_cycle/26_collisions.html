Collisions and clearance

FRobot checks the robot, the gripper and the part it carries against everything in the cell, all through the cycle.

Seeing a collision

Whatever is in a collision turns red in the 3D view:

Object Normally In a collision

Robot links

yellow

orange-red

Gripper, carried part (green), parts put down, cell elements, collision triangles

their own colours

red

Obstacle box

blue

red

As you play, scrub or jog, the colours follow the robot’s pose. The phase strip shows the same thing for each phase: see The phase strip.

Cell elements that overlap each other are red too, and the Frames panel’s status line warns ⚠ Cell elements overlap: A ↔ B.

Clearance

The planner keeps the arm, the gripper and the carried part a safe distance from elements with the role Other:

Distance What happens

40 mm

What the planner keeps where there is room

25 mm, then 10 mm

Where there isn’t room, the planner allows less

Under 10 mm

Never planned. A swing that comes this close is reported as a warning, (safe distance).

A swing that stays between 10 and 40 mm is not reported.

  • The Overturner is kept at 10 mm.

  • Everything else is only checked for real collisions, with no safe distance: the stations the robot works at (pickup, centering table, deposit, a loading machine), the robot’s stand, the obstacle box and the collision triangles.

To keep the robot well clear of a fixture, give it the role Other.

Fixing collisions

See Creating the solution. In short:

  1. Move what is in the way.

  2. recompute, with an RRT planner if a swing is blocked.

  3. Edit single waypoints last.
