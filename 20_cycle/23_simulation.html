Planning and checking the cycle

Simulation

Simulation

Watch the cycle in the 3D view to see how the robot moves. The controls are in the tray under the phase strip: move the mouse over the strip to drop it down. It is pictured in The phase strip.

The transport buttons

Button What it does

Play (▷)

Runs the cycle. From the end, it starts again from the beginning.

Stop (□)

Pauses where the robot is, which can be between two waypoints

Reset (↺)

Goes back to the first waypoint and puts the part back on the stack

Edit (✎)

Opens the Waypoints panel on the current waypoint: Waypoints

Continuous (⇄)

On: Play puts every part of the stack onto the deposit, one place after another. Off: one cycle.

Speed

1×, 4× or 8×

  • The scrubber moves through the cycle by time; its tooltip reads Cycle position — 4.2 of 31.6 s.

    • In a continuous run, dragging it back puts the grips back as they were at that moment.

    • In a single cycle it does not: the part stays held or released as it was. To see the part where it was at an earlier point, click a phase cell, or use the Waypoints panel.

  • Stepping: there is no single-step button. Use Prev and Next in the Waypoints panel, or click a phase cell.

The robot slows down where the wrist passes close to a straight line and where the arm turns a long way, so you can follow what it does.

The cycle time

The times in the phase strip and the scrubber come from fixed nominal speeds:

  • the axes at 120 to 360°/s;

  • straight moves at 200 mm/s;

  • 0.3 s for each grip or release;

  • 2 s for bending.

They are good for comparing one plan with another. They are not the cycle time the real robot will reach.

The second Waypoints panel

The repeat button at the top of the Workflow window opens a panel that is also titled Waypoints:

  • a button for each phase, and Simulate phase. The C and D buttons show even when the cell has no centering table;

  • Norm, Mid and Max speeds;

  • Full cycle, with a Continuous box;

  • the buttons ▶ ■ ↺ ✎.

It does not start playing by itself; use its ▶ or the tray.
